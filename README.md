# WorkOS

WorkOS is a buyer-owned professional-services operations platform that connects resource planning, approved work, effective capacity and commercial control. Its direction is an enterprise utilization product where every percentage has an explicit period, denominator, inclusion rule and authorized source.

> **Naming note:** WorkOS is the temporary product working name. Runtime branding and buyer terminology must remain configuration-driven.

## Product direction

- **Trust the numbers:** effective part-time capacity, calendars and once-only leave reductions; approved evidence and category inclusion; exact decimal calculations and reproducible exports.
- **Plan with context:** distinguish planned, approved actual and forecast work; expose bench, overload, demand and roll-off within the viewer's scope.
- **Recover clearly:** loading, empty, denied, unavailable and uncertain-save states must remain distinct. Preserve edits and reconcile uncertain commands before retrying.
- **Operate securely:** current identity/role/data scope, independent approvals, immutable audit evidence, verified database TLS, controlled migrations and tested recovery.
- **Stay buyer-owned:** isolated application/database deployment, configurable terminology and branding, provider adapters and documented handover.

These are product and acceptance commitments, not claims that every capability is already accepted on `main`. Financial valuation, billing eligibility and invoice readiness remain separate decisions; operational earned-management metrics are not statutory accounting recognition.

## Product boundaries

WorkOS v1 supports isolated buyer deployments, configurable organization and terminology, multiple engagement models, daily/weekly work capture, resource planning, approvals, effective-dated commercial terms, revenue/margin controls and invoice-ready exports.

WorkOS v1 is not a payroll system, statutory invoicing engine, general ledger, complete CRM, shared multi-tenant SaaS control plane or autonomous AI decision-maker.

## Shared login throttling

Migration `057c_wos04_shared_auth_throttle` stores expiring login counters in the buyer database. API replicas share the per-IP and per-input limits, including the Entra callback limit. Apply migrations before switching traffic, drain old API processes, and configure identical limits and trusted proxy settings on all replicas. Counters start empty on first upgrade. Storage failure returns HTTP 503 before authentication. Reverting to the previous application restores process-local counters and its readiness check rejects the newer migration ledger. Prefer forward correction and retain the migration.

### Request a full reversal of an exported line

In Invoice CSV export, inspect an exported or billed batch and retrieve its retained CSV. Finance preparers can select a line, check for an existing request and record the reason for a full reversal. Reopen the line to retrieve the receipt after an interrupted response. The request preserves the original export, control totals and occupancy. A policy administrator can explicitly enable named review stages for full-line reversals when recording an engagement policy. Check reversal reviewers on a recorded request to see the policy effective today in UTC. The check requires distinct reviewers with current access, independent of the requester and batch preparer. The original requester can submit the inspected request. WorkOS records the policy version and current downstream confirmation and delivers an action notice to the first named reviewer. Use Check reversal reviewers to recover the submission receipt. Reviewers can acknowledge the notice in their governed notifications inbox. Named reviewers can inspect the original line, amount, export checksum and current downstream confirmation in their inbox. Each stage records its decision and reason. Approval advances to the next named reviewer. Rejection ends the review and notifies the affected parties. The requester can retrieve the final outcome. After final approval, a finance preparer with the explicitly granted `commercial.reversal_eligibility.create` permission can inspect the original line and issue one approved-unbilled credit. The database uses the original currency and full line amount, checks the financial calendar for the UTC issue date, and retains the original export, final decision and credit receipt. In Invoice CSV export, select an engagement and choose **View issued credits** to find the signed amount, original batch and line, and register item. Finance preparers see credits only when they can view the entire original batch. Finance preparers with draft ownership permission can select up to 50 whole credits from the same original export, enter a reason, and reserve them in one correction draft. The register shows the occupying batch. Authorized preparers can cancel the draft to release every credit and retain its line history. Select **Inspect correction draft** to verify the owner, signed total, eligibility dates, original export checksum and versioned credit lines. A recorded owner access loss requires cancellation and a replacement draft before review. Under Invoice batch approvals, use **Find correction drafts** and inspect the signed total and credit lines before submitting. The effective engagement policy supplies the named stages. Each reviewer must be independent of the requester, owner and preparer and retain access to the original export. Final approval retains the credit reservations. Rejection returns the batch to Draft. In Invoice CSV export, use **Find credit exports** to accept or retrieve the approved correction CSV. Each signed credit line identifies its original artifact, checksum and line. Select the original batch and choose **Check exported credits** to see which lines were credited. In Invoice billing confirmation, select the engagement and choose Find exported credits. A scoped confirmer inspects the retained artifact, checks its negative total and records the downstream credit reference. The receipt is a manual attestation. Recheck exported credits on the original invoice to see which credit lines have a downstream attestation. To suspend new attestations, revoke the confirmation permission and retain historical receipts and artifacts.
