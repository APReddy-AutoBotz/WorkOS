# WorkOS Product Vision and Positioning

## Vision

Project-based service organizations should be able to control people, engagement delivery and commercial performance from one buyer-owned operational system without adopting an oversized ERP or surrendering their data to a shared SaaS tenancy.

## Product promise

> WorkOS connects workforce capacity, client engagements, work evidence, approvals, utilization, revenue and project margin in one governed operational layer.

## Ideal customer profile

WorkOS v1 targets approximately 25–500 project-based workers in consulting, software/IT services, AI and automation services, engineering, agencies, managed services, CRO/regulatory services and internal transformation groups.

Common conditions include:

- Multiple clients or demand owners.
- T&M, fixed-fee, milestone, retainer, managed-service or internal work.
- Spreadsheet-led allocations, timesheets or project financial control.
- Limited visibility of approved-unbilled work, margin and future capacity.
- Preference for isolated buyer-owned application, database and configuration.

## Value pillars

1. **Plan:** capacity, availability, demand, assignments, bench and roll-off.
2. **Capture:** daily/weekly work evidence with configurable policies.
3. **Govern:** role/data scope, approvals, locks, audit and data quality.
4. **Control:** rates, costs, budgets, POs, revenue, margin and invoice readiness.
5. **Prove:** reconciled reports, release evidence, buyer-owned deployment and operations.

## Differentiation

- Buyer-owned deployment and source package.
- Configuration before buyer-specific code changes.
- Multiple engagement models rather than T&M-only assumptions.
- Commercial snapshots that preserve historical rate/cost evidence.
- Invoice readiness without pretending to be a statutory invoicing or ledger system.
- Deterministic controls first; optional AI remains assistive.

## Positioning boundaries

WorkOS is not a payroll engine, full HRMS, CRM, task tracker, statutory invoice issuer, statutory revenue-recognition/accounting engine, general ledger or autonomous AI approval/calculation system. Integration contracts allow those systems to remain systems of record where appropriate.

## Working-name policy

`WorkOS` is temporary. Product display name, logos, colors, support identity and buyer terminology must come from controlled configuration so a commercial rename does not require feature-code changes.
