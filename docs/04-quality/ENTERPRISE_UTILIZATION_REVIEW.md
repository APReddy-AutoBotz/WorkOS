Governing WorkOS requirements remain authoritative; market features do not authorize new financial policy or scope expansion.

## External reference practices

The following official product documentation was accessed on the review date. Features and commercial packaging can change. No competitor code, screenshots, customer data or visual assets were copied.

| Reference | Relevant documented behavior | Practice to retain in WorkOS |
|---|---|---|
| [Float People Report](https://support.float.com/en/articles/4385599-people-report) | Date-range reporting distinguishes scheduled/logged views; capacity accounts for working hours and time off. Percentage displays distinguish scheduled/capacity, billable/capacity and billable/scheduled. | Name the denominator and period for every percentage. Separate utilization from the share of work that is billable. |
| [Runn Metrics Glossary](https://help.runn.io/en/articles/10356662-runn-metrics-glossary) | Actual, pending actual and scheduled future work are distinct inputs; forecast can incorporate missing-actual assumptions, and the documentation flags completion concerns. | Keep evidence completeness visible. WorkOS must not silently substitute a schedule for approved actual work. |
| [Resource Guru reporting guide](https://help.resourceguruapp.com/en/articles/4796610) | Report options explicitly control tentative/pending/waiting-list inclusion. Placeholder demand can affect ratios, with warnings and filtering. | Identify included states and demand separately from physical worker capacity; avoid unexplained aggregate percentages. |

These are comparable product practices, not identical calculation policies. In particular, WorkOS's approved-evidence and effective-capacity rules must not be replaced with another product's actual/forecast assumptions.

## Ordered follow-through

1. **Reconcile legacy project metrics.** Requirements WOS-CORE-003/008, WOS-RPT-002/007 and WOS-NFR-UX-003/005. Define one explicit period and label whether the measure represents whole-worker utilization or project contribution. Route any authoritative ratio through a scoped server projection using canonical evidence/capacity. Preserve legacy behavior only as clearly identified compatibility until its parity gate; do not silently relabel the old formula. Acceptance must include part-time workers, leave/holiday overlap, duplicate assignments, workers entering/leaving during the period, different last-submitted weeks, missing approval/category evidence, zero versus unavailable capacity, permission loss and scoped exports. Existing financial valuation and billability remain independent.
2. **Close remaining read/write recovery gaps.** Requirements WOS-NFR-OBS-005 and WOS-NFR-UX-005. Review ProjectMaster and remaining settings/catalog commands with actual failure/uncertain-write journeys. A service outage cannot become an empty business result or a success message. Preserve edits and reconcile uncertain outcomes before retries.
3. **Verify scale against the documented Standard profile.** Requirements WOS-NFR-PERF-001 through 008. Use representative records and 75 concurrent sessions; report read/mutation/report latency distributions, error rates and query plans. Audit broad list reads used for browser-side filtering against the bounded-result requirement. Do not infer this gate from fast unit tests or a successful build.
4. **Prove deployment recovery.** Requirements WOS-NFR-REL-006 through 009 and WOS-NFR-DPL-004/007. Rehearse backup, restore, migration-level validation and application recovery against an owned synthetic database; retain control totals and elapsed recovery evidence. Buyer infrastructure ownership and production sign-off remain separate.
5. **Complete release controls and terminology.** Requirements WOS-CORE-001/007, WOS-NFR-SEC-011/014 and WOS-NFR-RELSE-001 through 005. Finish the frozen full-source audit, dispose findings, resolve the known bootstrap-scan failures without blanket exclusions, reconcile stale status documents and rerun the release matrix at one immutable head. Do not equate draft PR count or green isolated checks with accepted readiness.
