These are configurable mechanics, not an adopted buyer rule. Overall-instance/business-day clocks, pause/resume, retrospective adoption, calendar conversion, automatic reminders/reassignment and other timing modes remain unavailable rather than guessed.

At a new instance's first pending-stage event, freeze the eligible prospective notice policy (policy created before the instance; effective UTC date not after its creation) into an immutable binding. New stages of that same instance keep that policy. Every canonical pending-stage event retains its actual database timestamp, stage/sequence, pinned source/policy/approver and exact due instant from the explicit delay. No binding or clock is backfilled for existing instances; their later stages remain unconfigured. Later policy edits affect only later new instances. A new clock never grants decision authority or changes approval/source state.
