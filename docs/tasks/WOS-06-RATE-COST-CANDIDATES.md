# Scoped rate and cost candidates and resolution preview

## Requirement lineage and boundary

PR-010 and WOS-COM-001–004, WOS-COM-014/015, CORE004/005/008 and IAM003/005/010 require effective-dated billing rates, confidential costs, deterministic resolution evidence, explicit currencies and auditable scoped changes. The commercial specification requires distinct billing, cost and commercial-maintenance permissions, explicit missing/conflicting outcomes and immutable source history. PR99 is the exact development base (`32cd43a9de9cf64637fbbf95594b09aed91adf18`).

The existing commercial module supports explicit scoped contract drafts/versions and engagement records, but no rate or cost sources or commercial-source canonical approval. Because commercial policy can require approval, this first slice **records protected candidate inputs and resolves a labelled preview only**. It cannot activate a rate, produce a calculation snapshot, make billing or approval decisions, infer buyer precedence or grant any real account access. Canonical commercial approval and protected authoritative rate adoption remain required before financial consumption. Draft resolution is not a substitute for that dependency.

## Explicit supported mechanics

- A candidate is scoped to the selected organization/legal entity and either a specific existing contract or a specific existing engagement for billing, or a specific existing scoped worker for costs. Its date interval, native currency, unit label, exact decimal amount and integer precedence rank are supplied explicitly; no seeded rates, order, unit conversion, FX, rounding, fallback or automatic effective selection is invented.
- Multiple candidates may match an explicitly selected engagement/worker and date. The preview derives the contract from the stored engagement, matches the explicitly entered native currency and unit, ranks explicitly supplied priorities, identifies source IDs and the matching basis, and returns **missing** or **conflict** when no candidate or more than one highest-ranked candidate exists. Cost and billing previews require their separate permissions and do not leak one another. Currency and unit are returned as recorded, without conversion or arithmetic.
- Candidate versions and material history are append-only, owned by active role, current scoped permission and organization/legal entity. Replay of an identical owned command returns the retained receipt; changed input, stale latest source or concurrent clashes fail safely. General audit receives reference metadata only; material amounts and entered reasons remain in protected domain evidence. No direct SQL/compatibility path may silently activate or revise a source.
- Existing contract, engagement, employee and work evidence must remain byte-stable; active account, held role, scoped commercial and worker visibility are revalidated. No new role/user grants, seed buyer policy or provider access. No promise of full dimension coverage, rate approval, commercial calculation, budget/PO or invoice readiness.

## Acceptance

Exercise explicit billing contract/engagement and cost worker candidates with native currency/unit/decimal preservation, date intervals, explicit precedence, missing/tie outcomes and original retained version after later changes; scoped/unauthorized cost and billing negatives, audit redaction, exact replay/changed-key/concurrent behavior, source and actor changes, immutable history, fresh and populated upgrade/replay migration, real React/API/PostgreSQL create/preview/receipt and owner/session fencing. Verify existing commercial/work histories remain unchanged. Batch development edits with targeted regressions; after freezing use a documented independent fresh-checkout applicable matrix and unchanged hosted CI. Formal review, integration, UAT and release remain unpassed.
