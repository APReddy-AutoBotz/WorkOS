## Selected conservative boundary

An authorized actor explicitly configures prospective named staged review for a selected engagement and submits an exact immutable T&M snapshot with a reason. No policy, approver, permission grant or eligibility is inferred or preselected. Named current approvers use a distinct opt-in billing-eligibility decision capability, hold current organization/legal-entity and engagement scope, cannot decide their own request, and are revalidated at each decision. Billing eligibility is distinct from rate-change approval, valuation creation and cost/margin access. Billing-scoped views and notifications must omit cost, margin and confidential rate information; source evidence may be internally pinned without widening user visibility.

Pending, intermediate, rejected, stale or invalidated requests produce no eligibility event or register item. Final approval atomically revalidates the exact snapshot, underlying approved work, retained client/contract/engagement and policy references, positive native-currency amount, current actor and source scope, and configured recipients. It creates exactly one immutable versioned billing-eligibility event and exactly one approved-unbilled item with source and decision evidence and a redacted audit. The event's amount is the retained snapshot's billable amount; no currency conversion, partial allocation, automatic inclusion, invoice, provider delivery or external scheduling is introduced. Historical policy, source and decisions remain unchanged. Exact same-key retry returns the original receipt; different payloads, duplicate source decisions, changed authority or concurrent conflicting attempts fail closed. There is no buyer configuration or account grant from migration or tests.

A scoped register may expose current available items with complete billing-safe source lineage. No invoice-ready line or batch path is introduced in this increment. Reversals, supersession, released occupancy, correction and adjustment paths remain unavailable until governed implementations exist; the future batch path must add database-unique current occupancy before accepting any line.

## Acceptance boundary

Prove an actual React/API/PostgreSQL configured named review from retained approved-work snapshot through pending, intermediate and final decisions to a single available register item. Cover unauthorized, foreign scope, self-approval and stale or revoked actor refusal; missing approval evidence; duplicate and concurrent submissions; original-request replay and uncertain responses; complete-recipient notification rollback; immutable source/history; billing-only data minimization; additive fresh/populated upgrade and replay with no grants. Preserve approved work, source rates and valuation snapshots. Use targeted development checks, a justified independent clean-checkout affected subset on the frozen head and unchanged hosted CI; do not publish before the dependency's exact-hosted acceptance. The full register across other commercial source types, invoice-ready occupancy/batches, corrections, export, integration, formal reviews, UAT and release remain open.
