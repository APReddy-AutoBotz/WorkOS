The original design and evidence notes below describe the earlier draft. The current mainline integration uses migrations 065b–065f and the acceptance gates in the WorkOS CI workflow.

## Selected conservative boundary

An authorized actor explicitly configures prospective named staged review for a selected engagement and submits an exact immutable T&M snapshot with a reason and an explicitly named current in-scope commercial change owner. The owner is retained on the immutable review; a changed owner requires invalidating the pending review and submitting a fresh review, and lost owner eligibility makes a pending decision stale. No policy, approver, permission grant or eligibility is inferred or preselected. Named current approvers use a distinct opt-in billing-eligibility decision capability, hold current organization/legal-entity and engagement scope, cannot decide their own request, and are revalidated at each decision. Billing eligibility is distinct from rate-change approval, valuation creation and cost/margin access. Billing-scoped views and notifications must omit cost, margin and confidential rate information; source evidence may be internally pinned without widening user visibility.

Pending, intermediate, rejected, stale or invalidated requests produce no eligibility event or register item. Canonical rejection notices resolve the requester, explicit commercial change owner and affected worker server-side, deduplicating identities without exposing confidential cost. Final approval atomically revalidates the exact snapshot, underlying approved work, retained client/contract/engagement and policy references, positive native-currency amount, current actor and source scope, and configured recipients. It creates exactly one immutable versioned billing-eligibility event and exactly one approved-unbilled item with source and decision evidence and a redacted audit. The event's amount is the retained snapshot's billable amount and its eligible date is the UTC date of the final decision, without backdating; no currency conversion, partial allocation, automatic inclusion, invoice, provider delivery or external scheduling is introduced. Historical policy, source and decisions remain unchanged. Exact same-key retry returns the original receipt; different payloads, duplicate source decisions, changed authority or concurrent conflicting attempts fail closed. There is no buyer configuration or account grant from migration or tests.

A scoped register may expose current available items with complete billing-safe source lineage. No invoice-ready line or batch path is introduced in this increment. Reversals, supersession, released occupancy, correction and adjustment paths remain unavailable until governed implementations exist; the future batch path must add database-unique current occupancy before accepting any line.

## Acceptance boundary

Prove an actual React/API/PostgreSQL configured named review from retained approved-work snapshot through pending, intermediate and final decisions to a single available register item. Cover unauthorized, foreign scope, self-approval and stale or revoked actor refusal; missing approval evidence; duplicate and concurrent submissions; original-request replay and uncertain responses; complete-recipient notification rollback; immutable source/history; billing-only data minimization; additive fresh/populated upgrade and replay with no grants. Preserve approved work, source rates and valuation snapshots. Use targeted development checks, a justified independent clean-checkout affected subset on the frozen head and unchanged hosted CI; do not publish before the dependency's exact-hosted acceptance. The full register across other commercial source types, invoice-ready occupancy/batches, corrections, export, integration, formal reviews, UAT and release remain open.

## Related owner-loss correction

A targeted synthetic regression showed that the existing candidate review rejected an owner while inactive but became actionable again after that owner was restored. That violates the previously selected fresh-review rule. The additive migration must retain a durable, immutable owner-loss marker whenever relevant account, role, permission, organization scope or existing worker-scope relationships remove current owner eligibility; restored access cannot reactivate the old review or an unadopted decision. Already adopted sources and historical decisions remain unchanged. Because prior versions did not retain authority-change history, existing non-rejected, non-cancelled and unadopted candidate reviews are conservatively marked as history-unavailable on upgrade and require a fresh review; this marker does not claim an actual loss occurred. Verify populated upgrade, replay, reversible scope loss and restoration, direct bypass refusal, concurrency and unchanged adopted records before removing the development-only migration block. No accounts, roles or permissions are granted by this migration.

## Implementation and limits

The local candidate adds a billing-safe React/API/PostgreSQL workflow with explicit policy, owner, review stages and a paginated available register; it does not create invoice lines or establish other source models. The database enforces a source-bound final decision, one event and item, redacted audit, canonical mandatory recipient completion, immutable records and current actor/source scope. A stale source is omitted from the available register; no protected work reopening or correction mechanism is added. Opt-in permission definitions do not create any account or role grant. Rejection recipients are requester, explicit change owner and affected worker, deduplicated server-side; informational completion respects in-app preferences.

The additive migration records loss at the committed authority boundary, so a temporary change reversed within one transaction does not falsely stale a review. Existing unadopted review histories lack authority-change evidence and are conservatively marked history-unavailable on upgrade; retained adopted sources and historical decisions are unchanged. Source loss, manager/worker scope, stage races, same-key replay, mandatory-notification and audit rollback, direct compatibility refusal, populated upgrade/replay and real browser workflow require the exact-source receipts before this increment can be called engineering-validated. Further approval domains, batching/occupancy, reversals, reconciliation, formal reviews, integration, UAT and release remain open.

Policy maintenance uses the latest recorded version for concurrency. Billing requests use an explicitly selected policy effective today in UTC. Upcoming versions remain visible in recent history. A missing current policy leaves the review unavailable. The optional policy selection read uses the existing role and engagement scope. Rolling back the application preserves policy and decision history.

WOS-APR-007 — Migration 065v validates the reporting periods covering each new billing eligibility event's UTC eligibility date. The decision requires Weekly coverage and permits Open, Submission Closed and Approval Closed periods only, including overlapping Monthly and Fiscal periods. A refusal rolls back the final approval, event and register item. Intermediate review, existing register reads and decision replay remain available. The T&M browser and milestone PostgreSQL journeys cover a refused final decision followed by a successful decision in an open calendar.

The trigger checks new events only. A populated upgrade preserves earlier decisions, amounts and reservations. If the application release is rolled back, keep the database guard active. An older client may show a generic error for a refused decision.
