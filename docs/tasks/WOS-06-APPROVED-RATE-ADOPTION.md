## Chosen conservative boundary

An actor who already has the existing scoped commercial-administration and billing-candidate write capabilities, or separately the existing cost-candidate write capability and worker scope, may explicitly adopt an exact billing or cost candidate **after** a complete configured review. The source candidate, its latest stream version, current owner version and eligibility, exact approval instance/policy and final approved transition must still match and be current at adoption. A new candidate or owner version or loss of current authority before adoption makes the action stale and requires fresh review. No owner, role, reviewer, financial value, conversion, priority or policy is inferred; permission definitions and user/role grants remain unchanged. Approval remains distinct from adoption, and a final decision does not automatically activate anything. Existing unreviewed candidates remain non-authoritative.

An additive, empty, append-only adoption ledger retains the exact candidate/owner/policy/approval/final transition, actor/role/scope, explicit reason, UTC time and original request identity, with atomic redacted audit. Identical original requests replay; changed input, source, owner or decision conflicts. To avoid inferring ordering from timestamps, the selected owner/policy cohort must contain exactly one non-cancelled review: a repeated or rejected review in that same cohort requires an explicit new policy version before adoption. Cancelled stale reviews do not count; an owner change creates a distinct cohort. Existing approved/adopted evidence is never rewritten when later ownership or permissions change. No live settings, buyer grants, backfill, invoice, billing eligibility, valuation, multiplication, FX or rounding is performed.

A separately labelled scoped adopted-source resolver handles the already-supported contract/engagement billing and exact-worker cost dimensions, explicit native currency/unit and caller-selected date. Only explicitly adopted sources are eligible; existing explicit priority produces one exact winner or a fail-closed missing/tied result. It returns the precise source/adoption/approval lineage rather than a calculated commercial line. Newer/different dimensions, cessation/corrections, future-version behavior, buyer policy, rate arithmetic and commercial pre-submission notices remain separate outcomes. Existing candidate preview stays visibly non-authoritative.
